Try clicking this button while the blocking calculation is running. Notice how it becomes unresponsive.

💡 What's Happening?
When you click the button, the JavaScript engine runs the loop synchronously. During this time, the browser cannot process any other events, making the UI completely frozen. This is the effect of blocking code.
⚠️ Warning: This calculation may take 10-30 seconds and will freeze your browser. You won't be able to interact with the page or browser during execution.
